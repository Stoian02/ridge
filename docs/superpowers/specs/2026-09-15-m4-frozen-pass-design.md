# Ridge Milestone 4 — Frozen Pass: Design Spec

**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Status:** Draft for the owner's review
**Parent spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-11-ridge-design.md` (binding). It refines §5 Surfaces (snow/ice), §6 Levels (the third level) and §11 "Later" (snow/ice, remaining levels).
**Builds on:** `master` at 675fb6e (Milestone 3 merged: cars, car select, surface feedback and sound, threaded terrain, loading screen)

---

## 1. Summary

Milestone 4 adds **Frozen Pass**, the third level: a climb over a snowy mountain pass and a twisty, bumpy, icy descent, about 2.1 km long. It brings:
- **Two new surfaces:** packed snow (slippery, slows you only a little) and ice (very slippery, keeps your speed). A **logs** surface for the bridge.
- **Grippier mud:** mud grip goes from 0.5 to 0.6, soft mud from 0.6 to 0.7. Their drag is unchanged, so mud still slows you as much; snow and ice become clearly more slippery than mud.
- **Two new trail structures,** each defined as level data and generated:
  - a **tunnel** (curving, lit, about 250 m) under the summit ridge
  - a **broken log bridge** over a frozen gorge, whose far half sits 0.7 m lower
- **Off-road ground can be any surface:** Frozen Pass's terrain counts as snow.
- **Build speed-ups** for the road and the terrain height field, so the longer level still loads in under 3 s on the phone.
- **Snow and ice strips** on the Test Ground, for comparing braking on each surface.

The car physics code is unchanged; only surface values and grip-table entries change (approved by the owner in the design conversation).

## 2. Decisions from the design conversation

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Route | Climb from a snowy valley over a pass, through a tunnel under the summit, then descend to the finish |
| Extras on the route | A partly broken log bridge with a small drop (not a jump), on the climb; the descent is bumpy (less than Muddy Valley) with a variety of turns and twists |
| Road cross-slope | Level from left to right everywhere; bumps span the full road width (no one-sided potholes or ruts). Climbs and descents are fine. |
| Snow and ice | Packed snow road the whole way; ice patches and short stretches, more on the descent and at the tunnel exit; dry tunnel floor |
| Tunnel | Curved, lit, about 250 m, you can't see the exit from the entrance |
| Log bridge | Step down mid-bridge: the far half sagged about 0.7 m, you roll off a ledge onto it |
| Grip | Mud 0.6 (was 0.5), snow 0.4, ice 0.15. Snow slows you down much less than mud. |
| Mood | Cold clear morning |
| Length | About 2.1 km, about 2 minutes |
| Architecture | Option A: tunnels and bridges as trail data (`TunnelDef`, `BridgeDef`) with their own builders |
| Resets | No automatic reset for falling into the gorge; the player resets manually. The existing auto-resets (flipped; fallen far below the map) stay as they are. |

## 3. The level

### 3.1 Place in the game
- **`levels/frozen_pass/`** holds the scene, `LevelDef`, trail, terrain and scatter definitions, and the generated curve.
- **Catalog:** the third level; under the existing rule it unlocks once Muddy Valley has been finished.
- **Surfaces line:** "Snow, ice, tunnel".
- **Star times:** placeholders of 2:15 (2 stars) and 2:00 (3 stars), set from the owner's first real runs.
- **Car unlock thresholds:** unchanged.

### 3.2 Route (about 2.1 km)

| Distance | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–350 m | Start in a snowy pine valley, gentle 3–5% climb |
| 350–800 m | Climb with two hairpins, 8–10%; two small ice patches |
| ~850 m | Log bridge (§5), 30 m, over a frozen gorge |
| 900–1150 m | Upper climb to the ridge through rock and snow, fewer trees |
| 1150–1400 m | Tunnel (§4), about 250 m, gentle S-bend |
| 1400–2050 m | Descent at 6–9%: esses, a hairpin, tightening corners; full-width bumps; ice patches, including right at the tunnel exit and in a few corners |
| 2050–2100 m | Finish in a snowy meadow |

- **Road shape:** generated by `tools/generate_frozen_pass_curve.gd` from straights and arcs, as for the other levels, and checked for parts passing too close. No banking.
- **Checkpoints:** 450, 900, 1420 and 1750 m, then the finish.
- **Bumps:** the descent uses the trail's undulation (full-width waves along the road) at about 0.12 m amplitude, plus full-width speed-bump-like rollers. There are no pothole clusters, rough sections or ruts on Frozen Pass, since those are one-sided.

### 3.3 Look
- **Mood (`GoldenHourMood` values):** a low, pale morning sun; pale blue sky; blue-white horizon; light valley haze.
- **Terrain:** snow-white (`dirt_color` about (0.9, 0.93, 0.97)), with grey rock on steep slopes.
- **Scatter:** pines with pale snow-dusted foliage in the valley, thinning toward the ridge; grey rocks; grey marker posts with orange reflectors. No broadleaf trees.
- **Road colours:**
  - snow: white with a slight blue tint
  - ice: glossy grey-blue, clearly different, so patches can be read ahead
  - tunnel floor: grey concrete
- **Ambience:** wind (stronger than the other levels, about 0.45), no birds.

## 4. Tunnel

### 4.1 Data (`TunnelDef`, `levels/trail/tunnel_def.gd`)
- `start` and `length` along the road (m)
- `inner_width`: the road width plus 1 m either side
- `height` 6 m
- `lamp_spacing` 12 m
- `cover` 8 m: the least ground above the ceiling
- `portal_length` 12 m: where the ground comes down to the portal face
- colours: wall, lamp
- **`TrailDef.tunnels: Array[TunnelDef]`.**

### 4.2 Builder (`TunnelBuilder`)
- **Shell:** a cross-section of straight walls rising into a rounded ceiling, placed every 2 m along the road. Wall vertices get a little seeded rock-like roughness, with dark rock vertex colours.
- **Collision:** a concave trimesh of the walls and ceiling.
- **Floor:** the normal generated road, with a concrete surface stretch over the tunnel's length.
- **Portals:** a rock face at each end, framing the arched opening, reaching up into the terrain.
- **Lamps:** small bright blocks along the ceiling, drawn unshaded and as one `MultiMesh` (one draw call).
- **Lighting:** the tunnel shell casts shadows, so the directional sun doesn't light its inside. No per-lamp lights, unless the phone check shows the inside reads too dark.
- **Camera:** the chase camera must stay below the ceiling inside. If the S-bend lets it rise above, the camera's height is capped while the car is in a tunnel.

### 4.3 Terrain over the tunnel (`TerrainField`)
- **Over the tunnel:** within the tunnel's range, the corridor carve doesn't pull the ground down to the road. The ground is raised to at least road height + tunnel height + cover, across the tunnel's footprint, and blends back to the natural mountainside around it.
- **At each portal:** over `portal_length` the ground falls from the ridge to the portal face, where the normal corridor carve takes over.
- **Height map:** the terrain stays a single height surface; the tunnel lies entirely beneath it.

## 5. Log bridge

### 5.1 Data (`BridgeDef`, `levels/trail/bridge_def.gd`)
- `start` and `length` (30 m)
- `drop` 0.7 m: how much lower the far half sits
- `gorge_depth` 6 m
- `ramp_length` 20 m: over which the road rises back after the bridge
- log diameter range 0.25–0.35 m, and a seed
- **`TrailDef.bridges: Array[BridgeDef]`.**

### 5.2 Builder (`BridgeBuilder`)
- **Logs:** laid across the road width, with slightly varied diameters, so it's bumpy. The first half sits level with the road; the second half, from a broken ledge at the middle, sits `drop` lower.
- **Collision:** one capsule or cylinder shape per log, tagged with the **logs** surface.
- **Drawing:** logs as a `MultiMesh`.
- **Details:** support posts down into the gorge; side rails, partly broken on the lowered half.
- **Road:** `RoadBuilder` builds no road mesh or collision across the bridge span.
- **Road profile:** `RoadProfile` lowers the road by `drop` from the bridge's middle to its end, then rises it back to 0 over `ramp_length` after the bridge, so the road rejoins the terrain.

### 5.3 Gorge (`TerrainField`)
- **Shape:** under the bridge the ground is cut down by `gorge_depth` across a band about 40 m wide, with steep rocky sides.
- **Floor:** tagged ice.
- **Resets:** falling in doesn't reset the car (it isn't below the kill height); the player presses Reset.

## 6. Surfaces and grip

### 6.1 Surface values (dry asphalt grip = 1.0)

| Surface | File | Grip | Rolling resistance | Drag | Sink |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mud | `surfaces/mud.tres` | **0.6** (was 0.5) | 0.1 | 40 | 0.06 |
| Soft mud | `levels/muddy_valley/soft_mud.tres` | **0.7** (was 0.6) | 0.08 | 25 | 0.04 |
| Snow | `surfaces/snow.tres` (new) | 0.4 | 0.04 | 10 | 0.03 |
| Ice | `surfaces/ice.tres` (new) | 0.15 | 0.01 | 0 | 0 |
| Logs | `surfaces/logs.tres` (new) | 0.7 | 0.03 | 0 | 0 |
| Tunnel floor | asphalt (existing) | 1.0 | 0.015 | 0 | 0 |

### 6.2 Grip table (`surfaces/grip_table.tres`)
Added entries:
- `rally/snow` 1.0, `rally/ice` 1.0, `rally/logs` 1.0
- `offroad/snow` 1.3, `offroad/ice` 1.05, `offroad/logs` 1.1

Unlisted pairs keep the table's default of 1.0.

Effective grip for the Rally Car and the 4x4:
- **Snow:** 0.48 and 0.55
- **Ice:** 0.18 and 0.17
- **Mud:** 0.79 and 0.85 (were 0.66 and 0.71)

### 6.3 Off-road ground
- **`TerrainDef.surface: SurfaceDef`,** default dirt. `TerrainBuilder` tags its collision with it instead of always dirt; Frozen Pass sets snow.
- **Unchanged:** Rally Road and Muddy Valley keep dirt, so their ground is unchanged.

### 6.4 Feel (`effects/surface_feel_table.tres`)
- **snow:** the dust spray in white (about (0.92, 0.94, 0.98)), plus a new `SNOW` rolling sound. No skid squeal.
- **ice:** no spray and no rolling sound. It skids, but with a muted squeal at half the skid volume.
- **logs:** the gravel rolling sound, no spray.
- **Sound:** `SoundSynth` gains a `snow` loop, a soft crunchy band-passed noise with gentle compression pulses, tested like the others. `TyreSoundLogic` gains a `snow` output.

### 6.5 Knock-on effects
- **Muddy Valley:** grippier mud makes it a little faster. Its scenarios and the car scenarios are re-run. Test ranges change only if a result leaves them, and the new numbers are reported.
- **Stars:** the owner's star times are not changed.

## 7. Build speed (phone load under 3 s)

The load benchmark showed Muddy Valley's remaining phone phases were road 1.0 s, field 0.65 s and shortcut 0.4 s (terrain is now 0.24 s). Frozen Pass is about a third longer and adds a tunnel and a bridge.

- **`RoadBuilder`:** computes each road chunk's vertices, colours and faces on worker threads, with the sampler's and profile's data copied into locals first. It then makes the meshes and bodies on the main thread in chunk order, with identical results. (Calling shared objects' methods from worker tasks serializes them; see `docs/notes/performance-m3b.md`.)
- **`TerrainField.generate`:** gets the same treatment for its carve loop, with results identical to the one-thread version.
- **Benchmark:** `debug/load_benchmark.gd` adds Frozen Pass.
- **Targets on the phone:**
  - Frozen Pass ≤ 3.0 s
  - Muddy Valley ≤ 2.0 s
  - Rally Road ≤ 1.3 s
- **Render budgets:** 60 fps, < 150 draw calls, < 300k triangles.

## 8. Test Ground
- **Snow and ice strips:** 14 m wide and 300 m long, beside the mud strip (snow at x = 45 m, ice at x = 60 m). The rough-asphalt lane moves further right to make room. New area signs.
- **Braking:** the new strips show how braking differs on each surface.

## 9. Testing

### 9.1 Unit tests
- **Surfaces:** the new and changed values, and the grip-table entries.
- **Feel and sound:** the feel-table entries for snow, ice and logs; the snow sound loop; the snow output of `TyreSoundLogic`.
- **Terrain surface:** `TerrainDef.surface` is used by `TerrainBuilder`.
- **Tunnel:**
  - its shell's width and height match its def
  - the walls have collision
  - lamps follow `lamp_spacing`
  - the field keeps at least `cover` above the ceiling along the tunnel
  - the ground meets the portals
- **Bridge:**
  - the logs span the road width
  - the far half is `drop` lower
  - the road profile's step and ramp
  - no road collision over the span
  - the gorge is `gorge_depth` deep
- **Speed-ups:** the parallel road and terrain-field builds give identical results to the one-thread builds.

### 9.2 Scenario tests
- **Frozen Pass drives:** each car finishes it with the surface-aware scripted driver and 0 resets; the times are printed for star targets.
- **Bridge:** crossing at a normal speed lands the drop upright.
- **Tunnel:** a car drives through without touching the walls, and the camera stays below the ceiling.
- **Test Ground braking:** from 60 km/h, the distance is ordered asphalt < snow < ice for every car.
- **Existing checks:** Muddy Valley and the car scenarios still pass, with the new numbers reported.
- **Level build:** Frozen Pass builds in under 3 s on the desktop, with its phase breakdown printed.

### 9.3 Checks by eye and on the phone
- **Screenshots:** the valley, the bridge, a tunnel portal, inside the tunnel, the descent, the Test Ground strips.
- **Phone:** load times with the benchmark; 60 fps in the tunnel; how the surfaces feel; star times.

## 10. Out of scope
- falling snow particles, frost or breath effects
- tyre tracks in snow
- water crossings (a later level)
- new cars
- the art pass
- per-lamp dynamic lights (unless the phone check needs a few)

## 11. Done when
- Frozen Pass is playable start to finish with every car, loads in ≤ 3 s on the phone, and holds 60 fps.
- Snow, ice, logs and the new mud grip feel as described. The tunnel and bridge are built from their data.
- All unit and scenario tests pass.
- The owner has driven it on the phone and set its star times. The branch is merged only when the owner says so.
